Paper Star: Andersen Tales

A paper theatre of Andersen tales

Fling a paper star, ricochet it off the walls and break the paper shapes before they reach the line. Each of the five elements beats the next. Twelve Hans Christian Andersen tales, each with its own boss, gilded cards to collect and a finale among the stars.

Support

Questions, a bug, or a purchase that did not arrive? Write to tranhothanhvu@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Purchases

Book I (four tales) is free. One in-app purchase opens Books II and III. After reinstalling the game or on another device, open Settings → Restore purchase. The purchase can be shared with your family through Family Sharing.

Sound

If there is no music, check that the silent switch on the side of your iPhone is off and that Music is on in the game.
