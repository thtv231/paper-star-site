Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Paper Star: Andersen Tales does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
  • Your progress, scores, cards and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the game deletes them.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple ID; the game only asks Apple whether the purchase is owned.
  • When you choose to share or save a card image, the iPhone share sheet is used and nothing is sent to us.
  • The game does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts. The card tilt effect reads device motion on the device only; it is never stored or sent.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
